# Giving AI design taste

Insights collected by [AI Insight](https://github.com/yassiEmp/ai-insight) from videos and pages, each with its source. Apply them; cite the source when it matters.

- **Collect inspiration from specialized websites to improve the design quality of AI-built websites.** Use sites like 21st.dev, Originkit, and Spell UI for components; Kawaii, Kinetics, and Animation Dictionary for text animation; Fontshare, Fonts In Use, and Fontesk for fonts. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Utilize specific platforms for 3D and scroll website inspiration.** Explore Scrolltide, GetLayers, and MotionSites for ideas on 3D and scroll effects. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Find color palettes and schemes using dedicated inspiration sites.** Browse Pinterest and Coolors for color ideas. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Gather inspiration for website sections and detailed elements.** Check Unsection, details.so, and Supahero for section and detail design ideas. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Discover diverse background designs for websites.** Visit backgrounds.supply Freebies, Gradient Lab, and Super Visuals for background inspiration. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Seek general design inspiration from broad platforms.** Use Pinterest, Awwwards, and curated.design for overall design ideas. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Source sound effects for UI elements from specialized sites.** Explore UI SFX and Bencho for sound inspiration. ([@hugomxnning](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Df--4CtAgrf/))
- **Use Prompt Motion to find motion graphics with accompanying prompts and skills to guide AI.** ([@itsmariahbrunner](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De4EwvahdMl/))

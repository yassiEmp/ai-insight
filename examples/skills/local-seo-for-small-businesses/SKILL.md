# Local SEO for small businesses

Insights collected by [AI Insight](https://github.com/yassiEmp/ai-insight) from videos and pages, each with its source. Apply them; cite the source when it matters.

- **Perform keyword research using Semrush and Google Ads Keyword Planner.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Track keyword trends using Google Trends.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Spy on competitors' website H1 tags, titles, descriptions, and lengths using Detailed SEO.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Analyze competitors' Google Business Profile categories, services, and photo counts using PlePer.** Use PlePer to see primary/secondary categories, number of services, and number of photos. ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Build websites using Claude Code.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Host websites using Cloudflare.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Track local search results over time using LeadSnap.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Secure guest posts using QGP.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Distribute press releases using PR Underground.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Obtain backlinks from local sources like the Chamber of Commerce or local sports leagues.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Track and index website performance using Google Search Console.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Optimize each page to rank using PageOptimizer Pro.** ([@camden.weup](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfGhqQvpiIR/))
- **Claim your business profile within ChatGPT's Business Hub to enhance visibility and interaction with potential customers.** Navigate to chatgpt.com/business-hub, search for your business, and click the plus button to add it. ([@edward.builds](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfk9FlGP7DY/))
- **Utilize ChatGPT Business Agents to answer customer questions and help them explore your offerings directly within ChatGPT.** Once your business is claimed, the business agent can be configured to interact with users. ([@edward.builds](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfk9FlGP7DY/))
- **Monitor insights from your ChatGPT business profile, including mentions, website clicks, and discovery topics, to understand customer engagement.** The Business Hub provides data on common themes in conversations where your website is cited. ([@edward.builds](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dfk9FlGP7DY/))
